# Circles

A short list of rooms in Prague worth going back to. Coffee, a drink, a table, a night — edited the way a playlist is edited, not the way a directory is searched.

## Who it’s for

Someone who already has a few places that feel like theirs, and wants the next one to be closer to that taste than to a ranking, a tourist square, or whatever is nearest.

## The list

Sign up. Answer five questions about how you actually go out. Pick five places you already like, and optionally a few that aren’t you. The places are real rooms in Prague.

From that, one circle is edited for you. Open a tile and you get photographs, a description, the traits that define the room, and why it was chosen. If you have been, say you loved it or that it isn’t for you. The list moves.

Other lists sit beside that one: coffee, drinks, clubs, Czech tables, Asian, wine, late nights. They stay ordered by the same taste.

## Where

Location is its own circle, not a playlist for every quarter.

- Near you, from the phone’s location.
- A hub: Holešovice, Karlín, Vinohrady, and a few others like them.
- Or a search for a street, a square, a room, or another quarter.

What comes back is within a short walk, still in your taste, with a walk time on it.

## Two ways to hold it

The same circle can be read as a magazine or as a phone app. A switch at the bottom moves between them.

The phone has three tabs:

1. **List** — the circle for you, as big tiles.
2. **Find** — where and what kind of room, chosen with bubbles.
3. **Curators** — lists kept in the spirit of a person (a writer, a sculptor, a restaurateur). Editorial circles, not a signed endorsement.

## How it should feel

Minimal, paper and ink, closer to a small magazine than to a ratings app. The words stay short. The photographs suggest the room. The point is a place you would return to.
